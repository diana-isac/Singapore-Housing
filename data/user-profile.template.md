# User Profile

## Dates

- target move-in: around August 9, 2026
- target move-out: between December 6, 2026 and December 10, 2026
- flexible move-in window: limited, should align closely with semester start
- flexible move-out window: some flexibility through December 10, 2026

## Budget

- ideal monthly rent: USD 1,000-1,200
- hard max monthly rent: USD 2,000
- working SGD reference for scoring: approximately SGD 1,350-1,620 ideal, SGD 2,700 hard cap
- pricing rule: evaluate using the actual 3-4 month stay period, not annualized pricing
- utilities included required: preferred, but not mandatory
- note: low price should be prioritized when quality remains good

## Housing Type

- preferred setup: private room in a shared apartment
- acceptable alternatives: dorm or student residence if privacy and semester compatibility are strong
- private bathroom required: no
- furnished required: yes
- air conditioning required: yes, at least somewhere in the apartment
- student-pass acceptance required: yes

## Room Requirements

- private bedroom required: yes
- bed size: anything bigger than a single bed
- desk in room required: yes
- natural light required: yes
- window required: at least one
- two-wall window exposure: preferred, not required
- fan in room: preferred if room AC is absent

## Bathroom Preferences

- dorm scenario: shared bathroom on the floor is acceptable
- apartment scenario: bathroom should preferably be inside the apartment and shared only with housemates

## Kitchen And Laundry

- kitchen: at least one accessible kitchen in the building or on the floor
- cooking importance: low
- laundry: washing machine required at least in the building; apartment-level preferred

## Commute To SMU

- ideal commute: 5-10 minutes walking
- hard max commute: 20 minutes by public transportation
- preferred transport mode: walking first, then public transportation
- ranking note: distance to SMU is currently the top priority, but there is flexibility within the acceptable range

## Location Preferences

- preferred neighborhoods: not yet specified
- acceptable neighborhoods: any area that meets the commute rule
- neighborhoods to avoid: not yet specified
- safety requirement: generally safe area

## Living Preferences

- with other students preferred: yes, ideally SMU students
- mixed-gender apartment acceptable: yes
- living with landlord acceptable: only if the setup is still peer-like and includes other residents
- co-living acceptable: yes, if legitimate and semester-friendly
- max roommates: flexible, but not an unusually crowded setup
- quiet/study priority: important
- overnight guests: preferred, but do not penalize listings for silence on this point
- strict guest policies: only penalize when there is an explicit restriction or clearly strict access control
- group reservation requirement: not ideal

## Lease Preferences

- short-term lease required: yes
- semester-only stay accepted by landlord: required
- extension option preferred: optional
- paperwork simplicity importance: important
- deposit preference: 1 month preferred, 2 months acceptable
- booking style: both direct booking and manual negotiation are acceptable

## Property Condition

- overall condition: must be clearly in good condition
- mold acceptable: no
- leak risk acceptable: no
- visible maintenance issues acceptable: no
- recent reconditioning: preferred within the last 2 years when disclosed
- trust and reviews: important

## Non-Negotiables

- private room
- desk in room
- landlord accepts semester stay and student pass
- maximum 20-minute public transport commute to SMU
- legitimate listing with no major scam signals
- bed suitable for two people
- natural light and at least one real window
- laundry access in the building

## Nice-To-Haves

- 5-10 minute walk to SMU
- living with other students, ideally SMU students
- bathroom shared only within the apartment
- apartment-level laundry
- two walls with windows or especially strong natural light

## Current Priority Order

1. distance to SMU
2. AC in the room or at least strong AC availability in the apartment
3. low price at good quality
4. semester-fit legitimacy and student-pass compatibility
5. room basics: private room, desk, window, bed bigger than a single
6. guest-friendliness only when there is explicit evidence of restrictive rules
