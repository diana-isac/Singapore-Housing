# SMU Guidance

This is the project-specific digest of:

- `references/smu_accommodation_tips.pdf`

## Practical Rules

- SMU does not provide exchange housing.
- Airbnb-style residential short stays should be excluded.
- Residential stays under 3 consecutive months are not legally suitable.
- City and MRT-adjacent housing usually costs more.
- "Fully furnished" often means basic furniture only.
- Utilities and Wi-Fi must be explicitly checked.
- Photos alone are not enough.
- Prefer live or virtual tours when possible.
- Do not pay a deposit before a signed lease.
- Do not rely on verbal agreements.
- Get all negotiated points in writing.
- Review deposits, maintenance, inventory, and early termination carefully.
- Always request payment proof.
- Document property condition at move-in and move-out.
- Apartment-level AC is operationally acceptable for this project; room AC is a
  preference, not a hard requirement.

## Consumer / Complaint References

The SMU guidance points to these escalation bodies:

- CEA
- IEA
- CASE
- Small Claims Tribunals

This matters because traceable operators and formal complaint pathways increase
trustworthiness.
