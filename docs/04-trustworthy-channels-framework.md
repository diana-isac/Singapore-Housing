# Trustworthy Channels Framework

The objective is not to find the most channels. The objective is to find the
smallest set of channels with the highest probability of legitimate,
student-suitable listings.

## Channel Types To Evaluate

- official university channels
- university housing offices or student services pages
- student communities with moderation
- established rental platforms
- co-living operators
- serviced apartment providers
- licensed property agencies
- expat and student housing providers

## Source Approval Checklist

Review each channel on these dimensions:

- ownership is identifiable
- company or organization has a real-world presence
- contact details are transparent
- platform has a clear moderation or reporting process
- listing format requires meaningful detail
- payment is not requested outside normal safeguards
- short-term housing is actually supported
- there is evidence the channel is currently active

## Source Status Rules

### Approved

Use freely for sourcing when:

- the operator is identifiable
- legitimacy controls are decent
- scam exposure appears manageable
- listings are suitable for short-term students

### Conditional

Use only with extra scrutiny when:

- the source is useful but crowdsourced
- moderation exists but is imperfect
- some listings may be legitimate and some may not

### Rejected

Do not use when:

- ownership is unclear
- scam patterns are obvious
- verification burden is too high
- terms are incompatible with student housing needs

## Evidence To Save For Each Source

- source name
- URL
- operator/company
- why it is trusted or not trusted
- risks
- decision
- date reviewed
