# Scam Prevention

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Never send money before identity and control of the property are verified.
- Never rely only on WhatsApp or Telegram screenshots as proof.
- Never trust urgency by itself.
- Never assume a nice website means the listing is real.

## Listing Red Flags

- rent is materially below market without a credible reason
- landlord refuses live viewing or live video verification
- identity is hidden or inconsistent
- photos appear reused, overly staged, or inconsistent with the address
- address is vague, partial, or withheld until payment
- payment requested to a person unrelated to the listing
- large upfront payment demanded before documentation
- lease terms are evasive
- pressure tactics are used

## Verification Evidence To Request

- full address
- landlord or agent full name
- agency name and registration details if applicable
- live video walkthrough or in-person viewing process
- sample lease or booking agreement
- written breakdown of rent, deposit, utilities, and fees
- proof they control the property

## Risk Grading

- `low`: strong identity, strong evidence, terms clear
- `medium`: mostly credible but some gaps remain
- `high`: major evidence gaps or behavioral red flags
- `reject`: scam indicators or unverifiable control
