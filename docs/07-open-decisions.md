# Open Decisions

These decisions are still needed before live sourcing can be done well.

## Budget

- ideal monthly budget
- hard maximum monthly budget
- whether utilities must be included in that number

## Room Type

- private room required or shared room acceptable
- private bathroom required or optional
- whether a room-level AC is preferred over apartment-level AC

## Commute

- maximum acceptable door-to-door commute to SMU
- preferred commute mode: walk, MRT, bus, taxi mix

## Geography

- preferred neighborhoods
- neighborhoods to avoid
- whether you prefer being near SMU, near nightlife, near green space, or near other students

## Living Style

- co-living acceptable or not
- living with landlord acceptable or not
- number of roommates tolerance
- quiet/study environment importance
- landlord rules tolerance: guests, overnight stays, cleaning rules, curfews
- whether you want only students or students plus young professionals

## Lease Terms

- exact latest acceptable move-in date
- exact earliest acceptable move-out date
- flexibility for staying until December 10, 2026
- deposit tolerance
- whether you can pay the full term upfront if required
- whether you can accept a no-refund or partially refundable deposit
- whether official registration or documentation is needed for your student pass

## Risk Tolerance

- whether conditionally approved sources can be used
- whether broker-listed options are acceptable
- whether dorm-style operators without perfect reviews are acceptable if the trust signals are otherwise strong

## Process Preference

- whether you want maximum breadth first or highest-trust sources first
- whether you want only top-tier options or a broader pipeline
- whether you want me to prioritize direct-bookable options over listings requiring manual negotiation
